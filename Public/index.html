Home of Everything ~ HR

Modern HR, built for Nigerian and African businesses.

At ~ HR Cottage , our mission is simple: make powerful HR systems accessible to every organisation.

Streamline recruitment, payroll, time & attendance, onboarding, performance and HR analytics in one place.

Reduce manual work, stay compliant with Nigerian regulations, and give leaders real-time insight into their people.

Whether you are a growing SME or a larger enterprise, HR Cottage helps you run HR that feels modern, not stressful.

Our Mission

To liberalise HR practice in Nigeria and across Africa by giving businesses tools that match their reality.

We combine deep HR expertise with simple technology so you can focus on people, not paperwork.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted HR technology partner for organisations in Nigeria and beyond.

We see a continent where HR is data-led, compliant and genuinely employee-centred.

Our Approach

We build around local rules and real-world workflows: Nigerian payroll, labour laws and African HR realities.

Light to deploy, powerful enough to scale with your organisation.

For HR & People Teams

Streamlined Operations

  • Automate leave, attendance and basic workflows without heavy IT projects.
  • Generate HR and management reports in minutes, not days.

Visibility & Control

  • See headcount, payroll trends and key HR metrics at a glance.
