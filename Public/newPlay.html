Understanding Nigeria PAYE Under the New Tax Act 2025

Pay As You Earn (PAYE) is the most common form of personal income tax in Nigeria. Under the New Nigeria Tax Act 2025, PAYE computation has been significantly simplified, but many employees and HR professionals still struggle to understand how their monthly tax is calculated.

This HR Cottage Free Nigeria PAYE Calculator was designed to remove confusion by applying the law exactly as written — no guesswork, no outdated rules, and no hidden assumptions.

What Changed in the Nigeria Tax Act 2025?

  • ✔ Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA) has been abolished
  • ✔ First ₦800,000 of taxable income is now fully exempt
  • ✔ Rent relief introduced (20% of annual rent, capped at ₦500,000)
  • ✔ New progressive tax bands ranging from 0% to 25%
  • ✔ Clear separation of allowable deductions before tax

Many online calculators still apply pre-2025 rules. This tool does not. Every calculation here strictly follows the current law.

Why Your PAYE May Look Different From Your Payslip

Many Nigerian employees discover that their PAYE differs from what appears on their payslip. This usually happens because:

  • ❌ Old tax rules are still being used
  • ❌ Rent relief is ignored
  • ❌ Pension or NHF is wrongly calculated
  • ❌ Allowable deductions are not fully applied
