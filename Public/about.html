About HR Cottage

Building modern HR solutions designed for Africa’s workforce and business realities.

Who We Are

HR Cottage is transforming how organizations manage people, payroll, compliance, and performance.

Our Mission

To fully liberalize HR practice in Nigeria and Africa by providing simple, affordable, technology-driven tools that empower HR teams, business owners, accountants, and employees to work smarter, stay compliant, and unlock real productivity.

Our Vision

A continent where every organisation — from micro businesses to large enterprises — has access to world-class HR systems that improve employee experience, boost efficiency, and build trust.

What We Do

We build powerful yet simple HR systems, including:

  • Payroll automation & PAYE computation (2025 Tax Act compliant)
  • Compensation & benefits administration
  • HR analytics & reporting
  • Learning & development workflows
  • Performance management systems
  • Recruitment & hiring tools
  • Employee data and records information system
  • Time & attendance tracking

Why HR Cottage?

  • Built for Nigerian HR laws, payroll logic, and compliance realities
  • Affordable and accessible to SMEs, NGOs, schools, and startups
  • Fully cloud-based — no installations needed
  • Designed to reduce manual work and increase HR capacity
  • Transparent tools like our Free PAYE Calculator build trust
