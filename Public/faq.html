Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about HR Cottage tools — including our PAYE Calculator, HR modules, pricing, and more.

Your Questions, Answered

If you don’t find your answer here, you can always contact us.

How accurate is the HR Cottage PAYE Calculator? +

The calculator is fully aligned with the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, including pension, NHF, rent relief, AVC, NHIS, life insurance, and all progressive tax bands. It computes annual and monthly PAYE automatically and is trusted by HR teams, auditors, accountants, and SMEs.

Does the PAYE tool support multiple employees? +

Yes. The tool includes a batch payroll mode where you can add multiple employees and download PAYE results in Excel, CSV, or PDF. Each employee’s rent relief, taxable income, and effective tax rate are also included.

Do I need to calculate pension or NHF manually? +

No. Pension (based on your % input) and NHF (2.5%) are calculated automatically. All statutory and allowable deductions are handled by the tool.

Is the rent relief automatically applied? +

Yes. Enter the annual rent paid, and the calculator applies relief equal to 20% of rent capped at ₦500,000 — as required by the 2025 Act.

Is HR Cottage free to use? +

The PAYE Calculator is completely free. Other HR modules are available under flexible pricing plans suitable for SMEs, schools, enterprises, NGOs, and consultants.

How do I book a demo? +

Visit the demo page, fill the form, and our team will reach out promptly to schedule a walkthrough tailored to your needs.

Does HR Cottage work for small businesses? +

Yes — our solutions are designed to support micro businesses (1–10 employees), small businesses, and growing enterprises. You only pay for what you need.

Can the HR Cottage system replace Excel payroll? +

Absolutely. The platform automates payroll, PAYE computation, deductions, attendance, and reports — eliminating the risks common with manual spreadsheets.
