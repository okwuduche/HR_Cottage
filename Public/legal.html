Legal & Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. This page explains how HR Cottage protects your data and operates responsibly.

Our Legal Framework

Transparent. Compliant. Secure.

Privacy Policy

HR Cottage collects only data necessary to provide our HR tools and improve user experience. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except where legally required.

  • We securely store information related to demo requests, contact forms, and product usage.
  • Your PAYE calculator entries are not saved unless intentionally exported by you.
  • We use cookies to enhance website performance and analytics.

Terms of Service

By using HR Cottage tools, you agree to:

  • Use the platform responsibly and lawfully.
  • Acknowledge that the Free PAYE Calculator is for informational purposes only.
  • Ensure all data you provide is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

Cookies Policy

HR Cottage uses cookies to improve loading speeds, analytics, and user experience. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not function optimally.

Data Protection

We comply with Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and global privacy practices. Sensitive data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
